# Archive

This directory contains superseded publications, promotional material, and
unvalidated concept notes. It is retained to preserve the public correction
record, not as current evidence.

## Historical publications

Files in [`historical-publications/`](historical-publications/) preserve their
original claims beneath prominent retraction notices. Those claims include
figures that were hardcoded or derived from inadequate data. Do not cite them
as results; use [`CORRECTIONS.md`](../CORRECTIONS.md).

## Concept notes

Files in [`concept-notes/`](concept-notes/) are speculative mathematical
analogies. They have not been validated as clinical, neuroscientific, alignment,
or therapeutic methods.

## Artifacts

[`artifacts/`](artifacts/) contains PDFs, TeX, and submission bundles generated
from the historical publications. They are archived for provenance and may
repeat retracted claims.
