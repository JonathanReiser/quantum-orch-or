Before you begin

LOCAL PILOT

This prototype asks you to make 12 strategic choices against a scripted opponent. It records choices, response times, outcomes, and randomized study assignments in your browser.

No data is transmitted. Results remain in this browser unless you choose to download them. Do not enter your name, email, wallet address, or other identifying information.
This is not an approved human-subjects study. Do not recruit participants or present its output as research evidence until the protocol receives any ethics review required by your institution or jurisdiction.
